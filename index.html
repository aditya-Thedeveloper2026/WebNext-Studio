08 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions? We've got answers.

A few things you might want to know before starting your project.

What kind of projects do you work on?

We work on business websites, personal portfolios, web applications, practical AI integrations, automation, and website maintenance.

How does the project process work?

We begin by understanding your requirements, then plan and develop the solution, share progress for review, and prepare the final handover.

Are domain and hosting included?

Domain registration and hosting are separate expenses. Their requirements and costs are discussed before the project begins.

Can I request a custom solution?

Absolutely. If your idea doesn't fit a standard package, contact us with your requirements and we'll discuss a suitable approach.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Maintenance and ongoing support can be discussed separately depending on your needs.
